Yoga & Nutrition

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how Yoga & Nutrition (the “App”) handles information when you use its yoga, nutrition, tracking, reminder, and wellness assistant features. It describes the current offline app and this privacy policy website.

The App processes your profile and wellness information on your device. The current App does not send this information to developer-operated servers, advertising services, analytics services, or external AI providers. Device backups and information you choose to share are explained below.

1. Information stored in the App

Depending on the features you use and the information you provide, the App stores:

  • Local account information: name, email address, account creation time, and a salted password hash. Your password is not stored as plain text.
  • Profile and wellness details: age, gender, height, weight, activity level, goals, dietary preferences, allergies, health notes, yoga experience, and preferred practice and bedtime settings.
  • Activity records: yoga sessions, duration, completion and feedback, meal logs and swaps, nutrition estimates, water intake, weight entries, favorites, and grocery selections.
  • Assistant and settings data: messages and responses, reminder preferences, notification history, and local sign-in state.

Accounts are specific to the device. Entering an email address does not create a cloud account or enable cloud synchronization. Password recovery uses a local notification rather than an email message.

2. How information is used

The App uses this information to manage local sign-in, personalize yoga and meal plans, calculate wellness estimates, track progress, provide local assistant responses, and schedule reminders. The assistant uses on-device logic and local context; it does not send chats to an external AI service.

3. Permissions and notifications

The App may request notification permission and, on supported Android devices, permission to schedule exact alarms. It also restores reminders after a restart and may vibrate for notifications. You can change notification and reminder settings in the App or your device settings.

Notifications may reveal reminder details or password reset codes on your lock screen. Use your device's notification privacy settings to control previews. The current App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, HealthKit, or Health Connect.

4. Sharing and third parties

The current App does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and the developer does not receive your locally stored profile or wellness records through the App. We do not sell your App data.

If you use a share feature, the content you select is passed to an app or recipient you choose through your device's sharing interface. Their privacy policies apply after sharing. If you contact support, the recipient receives the information you include; avoid sending passwords or unnecessary health details.

5. Device storage, backups, and security

App records are stored in a local database within the App's storage area. Password hashing does not encrypt all profile, health, or chat records. Protect your device with a passcode and keep its software up to date.

Android backup is enabled in the current App. Depending on your operating system, device settings, and backup provider, App data may be included in cloud backups, device transfers, or other device backups. The App does not operate its own cloud backup service. Backup providers control the handling and retention of those copies under their policies.

6. Retention, access, and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you remove them or the App's data. Signing out does not delete your account or history. You can review your records and edit available profile fields in the App.

  • Android: open device Settings > Apps > Yoga & Nutrition > Storage, then choose Clear storage or Clear data. This removes all local accounts and records for the App on that device. Labels vary by device.
  • iOS: choose Delete App in device storage settings to remove the App and its local data. Offload App retains its data.

Uninstalling normally removes local App data, but existing backups and previously shared copies may remain. Manage backups separately through your device or backup provider; restoring a backup may restore old records. The developer cannot remotely access or delete data that exists only on your device. Contact us for help with these steps.

7. Children and family use

The App includes age-based content, including programs for children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use, review the information entered, and control sharing and notifications. Children's profile and activity information follows the same local processing and backup practices described in this policy. Avoid entering unnecessary identifying or sensitive details about a child.

8. This privacy policy website

This policy page may be hosted on GitHub Pages for testing or Google Firebase Hosting. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. When you visit a Firebase-hosted version, Google may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and operate the website; see Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.

This page contains no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms, or cookies added by us. Hosting this page does not upload your App records to GitHub or Firebase.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the App's data practices change and revise the date above. New features involving cloud accounts, analytics, advertising, or external AI services would require an updated policy and any necessary disclosures or permissions.
